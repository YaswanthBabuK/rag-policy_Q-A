REFUND POLICY

Our refund policy is designed to ensure customer satisfaction while maintaining fair business practices.

Eligibility for Refunds:
Products must be returned within 30 days of purchase
Items must be in original condition with tags attached
Digital products are non-refundable once downloaded
Custom or personalized items cannot be refunded

Damaged or Defective Items:
If you receive a damaged or defective product, we will provide a full refund or replacement at no additional cost. Please contact us within 7 days of receiving your order with photos of the damage.

Refund Process:
1. Contact our customer service team with your order number
2. We will provide a return shipping label (free for damaged items)
3. Ship the item back to us
4. Once we receive and inspect the item, we'll process your refund
5. Refunds typically take 5-10 business days to appear in your account

Partial Refunds:
In some cases, we may offer partial refunds for items that are returned in used condition or without original packaging.

Non-Refundable Items:
Gift cards
Downloadable software
Health and personal care items
Sale or clearance items marked as final sale

For questions about refunds, email support@company.com or call 1-800-REFUNDS.
