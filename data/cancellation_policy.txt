ORDER CANCELLATION POLICY

We understand that plans change. Here's how to cancel your order.

Cancellation Before Shipment:
Orders can be cancelled free of charge before they ship. 
Contact us immediately at orders@company.com with your order number. 
If your order hasn't entered our shipping process, we will issue a full refund with no cancellation fees. 
The refund will be processed within 1-3 business days.

Cancellation After Shipment:
Once an order has shipped, it cannot be cancelled through our system. 
However, you have two options:

Option 1 - Refuse Delivery:
Simply refuse the package when the carrier attempts delivery. 
The package will be returned to us automatically. 
Once we receive the returned package, we'll issue a full refund. 
This process may take 2-3 weeks depending on shipping times.

Option 2 - Standard Return:
Accept the delivery and follow our standard return process. 
You have 30 days from the delivery date to return the item for a full refund. 
See our Refund Policy for complete details on the return process.

Partially Shipped Orders:
If your order contains multiple items and some have already shipped, you can cancel only the items that haven't shipped yet. 
Items that have already shipped must be returned following our standard return process.

Custom or Made-to-Order Items:
Custom or made-to-order items cannot be cancelled once production begins. 
Production typically starts within 24 hours of placing your order. 
If you need to cancel a custom order, please contact us immediately. 
We may be able to cancel if production hasn't started yet.

Subscription Orders:
Subscription orders can be cancelled at any time before the next billing cycle. 
There is no penalty for cancellation. 
The remaining portion of your current subscription period will be honored.

Digital Products:
Digital products cannot be cancelled after they have been downloaded. 
If the product hasn't been downloaded yet, please contact us immediately for cancellation.

How to Cancel Your Order:
1. Email orders@company.com with your order number
2. Include the reason for cancellation (optional but helps us improve)
3. We'll send you a confirmation email within 24 hours
4. Refund will be processed according to your original payment method

For urgent cancellation requests, call 1-800-555-CANCEL (available Monday-Friday, 9 AM - 6 PM EST).
