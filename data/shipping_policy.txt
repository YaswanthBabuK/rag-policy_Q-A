SHIPPING POLICY

We ship to all 50 US states and over 100 countries worldwide.

Shipping Methods and Delivery Times:

Standard Shipping (US):
Cost: $5.99 flat rate
Delivery time: 5-7 business days
Available for all orders under $50

Expedited Shipping (US):
Cost: $12.99 flat rate
Delivery time: 2-3 business days
Order by 2 PM EST for same-day processing

Express Shipping (US):
Cost: $24.99 flat rate
Delivery time: 1-2 business days
Order by 12 PM EST for same-day processing

Free Shipping:
Available on all orders over $50
Standard shipping speed (5-7 business days)
Automatically applied at checkout

International Shipping:
Shipping costs are calculated at checkout based on destination
Delivery time: 7-21 business days depending on country
Customers are responsible for any customs fees and import duties

Order Processing:
Orders placed before 2 PM EST ship same day
Orders placed after 2 PM EST ship next business day
We do not ship on weekends or holidays

Tracking Information:
All orders include tracking information sent to your email once the package ships. 
You can track your order through our website or the carrier's website.

Shipping Restrictions:
We cannot ship to PO boxes for expedited or express shipping. 
Some items cannot be shipped internationally due to customs regulations. 
Military APO/FPO addresses are supported with standard shipping.

Lost or Damaged Packages:
If your package is lost or damaged in transit, please contact us within 7 days. 
We will work with the carrier to resolve the issue and ensure you receive your order.

For shipping questions, contact shipping@company.com or call 1-800-555-SHIP.
