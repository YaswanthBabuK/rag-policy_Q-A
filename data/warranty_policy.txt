WARRANTY POLICY

We stand behind the quality of our products and offer comprehensive warranty coverage.

Standard Warranty Coverage:
All products come with a 1-year limited warranty from the date of purchase. 
This warranty covers manufacturing defects and workmanship issues. 
The warranty does not cover normal wear and tear, accidental damage, or misuse.

Extended Warranty Options:
We offer optional extended warranty plans for 2 or 3 years. 
Extended warranties must be purchased within 30 days of your original purchase. 
Extended warranty plans cover the same issues as the standard warranty and can be purchased on our website or by calling customer service.

What's Covered:
Manufacturing defects, faulty materials, workmanship issues, and electronic component failures under normal use conditions.

What's Not Covered:
Accidental damage, water damage, cosmetic damage that doesn't affect functionality, normal wear and tear, damage from misuse or abuse, damage from unauthorized repairs, and lost or stolen items.

How to Make a Warranty Claim:
1. Contact our warranty department at warranty@company.com
2. Provide your order number and description of the issue
3. Include photos or videos demonstrating the problem
4. We'll review your claim within 2 business days
5. If approved, we'll provide a prepaid shipping label
6. We'll repair or replace the item within 10 business days

Warranty claim decisions are made at our discretion based on the information provided. 
We reserve the right to request additional information or to inspect the product before approving a claim.

For warranty questions, email warranty@company.com or call 1-800-555-WARRANTY.
